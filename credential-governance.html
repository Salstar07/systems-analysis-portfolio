01 / THE PROBLEM

What needed
to change?

A credential manager must support the actual access model and recovery arrangements—not simply advertise security features. Different permission and recovery scenarios require separate verification.

02 / THE CONTRIBUTION

What I worked on.

Prepared and executed bounded evaluation scenarios with a second user, recorded positive and denied-access behaviour, observed security and audit functions, and documented restoration, ownership and rollout dependencies in a reconciled register.

SCOPE OF CONTRIBUTION

These accounts distinguish work I contributed from wider team outcomes and explain which results were actually demonstrated. Original employer and university records remain with their authorised owners.

03 / THE JUDGEMENT

Decisions that mattered.

01

Test both allow and deny

Confirm an ordinary member can access assigned material and cannot see a restricted collection in the evaluated configuration.

02

Treat revocation as conditional

Removing a vault grant does not retract knowledge of previously copied credentials or automatically terminate third-party sessions.

03

Separate test success from adoption

Document current licensing, hosting, ownership and independent recovery gaps before broader operation.

05 / OUTCOME & LIMITS

What I can
defend.

REPORTED OUTCOME

A structured comparison grounded in observed controls and documented limits, with unresolved backup-owner, offline-cache and rollout matters referred for appropriate decisions.

BOUNDARY / LIMITATION

Some controls were not tested end to end, including independent sole-owner recovery and offline-cache revocation. This is a bounded pilot, not a certification of organisation-wide security.

WHAT COMES NEXT

Close remaining governance decisions, confirm operating ownership and maintain a controlled rollout plan.
