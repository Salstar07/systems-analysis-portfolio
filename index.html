How to read project evidence

Indicators apply to individual claims, not an entire project's production readiness. Each case identifies the supporting source and its limits.

Verified
Supported by test results, records or owner confirmation, with the basis stated.
Documented
Recorded in an analysis, specification or technical account. Original source records may still need review.
Illustrative
Independently created diagrams or synthetic data, separate from the employer's systems.
Pending verification
Details or outcomes that need confirmation or supporting evidence.

R-01 / REQUIREMENT

Missing availability must remain unknown. Scheduled work stays visible; a full total is withheld when any source record is missing.

D-01 / DESIGN CHOICE

Represent missing values as null, distinguish confirmed zero, and calculate whole minutes to avoid rounding drift.

T-01 / CHECK

With Analyst C’s availability missing and 8h scheduled, expect 2/3 known coverage, an unknown row balance and no full capacity total.
