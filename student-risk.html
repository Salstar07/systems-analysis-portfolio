SCOPE OF CONTRIBUTION

These accounts distinguish work I contributed from wider team outcomes and explain which results were actually demonstrated. Original employer and university records remain with their authorised owners.

03 / THE JUDGEMENT

Decisions that mattered.

01

Define the data model first

Resolve student identity, source structure, completeness and reporting definitions before interpreting risk.

02

Make insights understandable

Reporting views should help staff identify patterns without obscuring uncertainty or substituting for professional support decisions.

03

Evaluate predictions carefully

A predictive component needs a defensible method, explicit evaluation and limitations; intended early intervention is not itself a measured outcome.
