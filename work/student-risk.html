01 / BUSINESS PROBLEM

Bring the information
into context.

Student information distributed across multiple sources makes consistent academic-risk reporting difficult. Staff need understandable reporting definitions and joined-up information before patterns can support a useful student-service decision.

Project objectives

  • Consolidate relevant student information into a coherent reporting model.
  • Develop interactive reporting around stakeholder questions and shared definitions.
  • Explore predictive analytics as an academic concept, with evaluation and limitations made explicit.
02 / MY CONTRIBUTION

Where I contributed.

I collaborated with university stakeholders, including the Data Analytics Lead, on requirements, multi-source student data consolidation, data modelling and reporting design. I applied business analysis and project coordination to support the team’s academic deliverable.

  • Worked with stakeholders to clarify reporting needs and the intended student-support context.
  • Contributed to multi-source data integration and modelling for a unified reporting view.
  • Supported interactive reporting design and the team’s academic analysis, while distinguishing the predictive concept from a validated operational model.
SCOPE OF CONTRIBUTION

This is a university team project. The account describes my contribution and the project purpose; it does not establish sole authorship, a production deployment or measured student outcomes.

03 / REQUIREMENTS

Define what the report
needs to answer.

The analytical approach linked stakeholder questions to source structure, student identity, data completeness and reporting definitions. The data model provides the foundation for interpreting patterns; a visual dashboard alone cannot resolve inconsistent underlying information.

  • Consistent information. Reconcile student identity and source relationships before combining records for analysis.
  • Understandable reporting. Make the reporting definitions and intended staff questions clear.
  • Responsible interpretation. Keep exploratory risk indicators and predictive concepts separate from proven outcomes.
04 / SOLUTION DESIGN

From distributed sources
to reporting insight.

The academic work brought together multi-source data consolidation, data modelling and interactive reporting design. Predictive analytics formed part of the project concept, but the public account does not supply the original model, evaluation method or accuracy results.

Decisions & trade-offs

01

Resolve the data model before interpreting risk

Identity, source completeness and reporting definitions determine what a combined view can support.

02

Keep reporting understandable to stakeholders

Interactive views should help staff explore patterns while retaining uncertainty and the need for professional judgement.

03

Require evidence for predictive claims

A predictive component needs a documented method, evaluation and limitations. An intention to support earlier intervention is not a measured improvement in retention.
